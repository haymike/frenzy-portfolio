Dreamy forestStudio

Client: Syanne

Year: 2025

Role: Set Fabrication | Set Design


Designed and built by us in collaboration with Syanne, this immersive forest studio is inspired by the lush rainforest meets Ghibli whimsy. Layers of florals, mosses, and greenery climb through the space, creating a rich, otherworldly landscape. Created as a rentable photo studio.
