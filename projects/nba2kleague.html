NBA 2K LeagueMODULAR backgrounds kits

We designed a modular backdrop kit system for the creators of the NBA 2K League, building on the league’s recent rebrand with a bold color palette and eclectic iconography that sets 2K apart from its NBA counterpart. Designed, produced, and deployed entirely remotely, the kits were shipped nationwide as compact, easy-to-assemble systems - allowing each creator to personalize their setup while maintaining a cohesive, broadcast-ready visual identity.

What this system delivers:

Remote-first production- designed, approved, and deployed without on-site installation

Nationwide scalability- identical kits shipped to talent across the U.S.

Customizable & modular- interchangeable components allow for individual flair within a unified look

Compact & affordable- designed for easy shipping, storage, and home studio use

Stream-ready- optimized for live broadcast, webcams, and small studio footprints

Brand consistency at scale- one system, many creators, one visual language
