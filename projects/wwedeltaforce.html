WWE x Delta Force

Client: XLA Studios

Year: 2026

Role: Production Design | Wardrobe Styling | Custom Fabrication


We led the production design for the Burst Fest promo, blending stylized game elements with gritty, lived-in gym textures.

Live Forever.


Custom Prop Fabrication

Hero props included a custom-built Cato statue, branded dog tags featuring the game’s logo, and a series of wrestling-meets-tactical artwork pieces.

Wardrobe Styling

Wardrobe centered around two primary looks, meant to blend the world of WWE with the world of Delta Force. A simple, black training look on one side, eventually evolving into a full tactical look with layered combat elements.
